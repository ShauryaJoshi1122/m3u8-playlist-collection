IMPORTANT
- The master playlist points to the upstream all-channel index; it does not copy or host media.
- Category and country lists overlap with the all-channel index, so combining all 12 source URLs as separate entries would not create a clean channel-by-channel playlist and may cause duplicates or fail in players that do not support nested playlists.
- These are playlist index URLs, not direct channel stream URLs.
- Upstream channel availability and terms may change. Use only streams you are authorized to access.
